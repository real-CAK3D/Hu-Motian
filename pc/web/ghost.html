GHOST HUNTER

Hu-Motian's radar, ready for ghost hunting. Three modes:

  • Real: only what the radar truly detects. Tape the radar to the back of the phone, gold antenna side facing out, and turn slowly. Detections stay on screen in the direction you found them.
  • Ghost: staged encounters. Sweep the room to find the reading, aim at it, and hold TRAP before it gets you.
  • Hunt: go outside and use GPS to track down a ghost that wanders, and sometimes runs. Get close and it shows up.

Jump scares flash the screen and play a loud scream. Turn them off for anyone sensitive to flashing light or sudden noise.
