GHOST HUNTER

Paranormal-investigation kit for Hu-Motian. Pick a device from the bar at the bottom: night vision, thermal, an SLS camera, a laser grid, a K-II meter, an EMF/temperature meter, a REM pod, a spirit box, a word box, an EVP recorder, or the radar.

  • Real: nothing is faked. Only the radar and your kit sensors drive the devices. Tape the radar to the back of the phone, gold side out.
  • Ghost: a staged haunting, made up by the app. It moves around the room you're in and stays where it is when you look away. Find it, then hold TRAP before it comes for you.
  • Hunt: an outdoor GPS chase that ends in a staged encounter.
  • Bust: be the exterminator. Find it with the ghost meter, hold FIRE to wrangle it in the beam, throw the trap, drag it in and get paid. Callouts show up on the map.

Headphones make it much better: footsteps, knocks and whispers come from where the apparition actually is.

Jump scares flash the screen and play a loud scream. Turn them off for anyone sensitive to flashing light or sudden noise. The thermal view is an effect made from camera brightness; phones have no heat sensor.
